Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding Generative Engine Optimization.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the technical and semantic structuring of your website's data so that AI models (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) recognize your company as the authoritative answer and cite your brand in generated responses.

How does SKX Labs implement these fixes?

We inject custom nested JSON-LD schema graphs, publish root llms.txt files for AI bots, and restructure your core pages with direct BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) answers that match query fan-out sub-queries.
